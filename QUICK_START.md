# Quick Start Guide

This file gives you the shortest path from idea to published content.

## Step 1: Pick the current week

Use one of these trackers:
- `tracker/content_tracker_w1.csv`
- `tracker/content_tracker_w2.csv`
- `tracker/content_tracker_w3.csv`
- `tracker/content_tracker_w4.csv`

Open it in Google Sheets or Excel.

## Step 2: Review last week’s metrics

Before planning a new week, check:
- What content got the most saves?
- What series had repeat viewers?
- What content converted to follows?
- What content felt authentic and community-based?

## Step 3: Use the planning prompt

Open:
- `prompts/weekly_planning_prompt.md`

Paste your tracker data into the prompt and ask the AI to generate a plan.

## Step 4: Generate content ideas

Open:
- `prompts/idea_generation_prompt.md`

This gives you 10–15 content ideas with hooks, visuals, platform placement, and calls to action.

## Step 5: Draft the long-form asset

Open:
- `prompts/long_form_script_template.md`

Use it for:
- testimony
- devotional
- behind-the-bar breakdown
- music performance recap
- community update

## Step 6: Repurpose the long-form asset

Open:
- `prompts/repurposing_prompt.md`

This breaks one asset into 8–12 platform-native clips.

## Step 7: Write captions and hooks

Open:
- `prompts/caption_generation_prompt.md`

Use this to write final captions and content-specific CTAs.

## Step 8: Publish using the schedule

Open the relevant schedule file:
- `calendar/week_1_schedule.csv`
- `calendar/week_2_schedule.csv`
- `calendar/week_3_schedule.csv`
- `calendar/week_4_schedule.csv`

## Step 9: Track metrics

Update your tracker after 24–72 hours.

Then review:
- `analytics/kpi_dashboard.md`
- `analytics/weekly_review_template.md`

## Step 10: Repeat

Consistency beats bursts.

The goal is not to go viral every day. The goal is to build a system that compounds over 8–12 weeks.

---

## Weekly Routine

Monday: plan
Tuesday: record
Wednesday: edit + schedule
Thursday-Sunday: publish + engage

## Weekly Content Goal

Aim for:
- 1 core long-form idea
- 8–12 short-form clips
- 2–3 carousels or text posts
- 3–5 story slides
- 1 collaborative post
- 1 CTA beyond follow
