# Faith Before Fame

## Purpose
Teach a lesson from the intersection of faith, purpose, and platform culture.

This is where J.E.R.K. can speak directly to aspirational creators and believers who are trying to stay rooted in purpose.

## Format
- Weekly teaching or opinion video
- Focus on identity, spirituality, or creative pressure

## Best Hooks
- "The industry almost convinced me to change my message."
- "This is what I learned before the platform noticed me."
- "Faith before fame is not a slogan — it’s a discipline."

## CTA
Save this for later or tag someone wrestling with purpose.

## Visual Style
- Talking head with consistent background
- Light overlays with scripture or principle cards
- Direct voice, minimal distractions
