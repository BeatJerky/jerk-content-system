# Behind the Bar

## Purpose
Show the process behind the song: writing, recording, producing, and revising.

This series humanizes the work and gives the audience a window into creation.

## Format
- Weekly studio session or desk footage
- The process of writing a verse
- Performance or voiceover with B-roll

## Best Hooks
- "This is what changed my creative process."
- "I almost cut this verse."
- "This is how I turned pain into a bar."

## CTA
Follow for the next studio session or comment on what you want to hear next.

## Visual Style
- Studio B-roll
- Mic close-ups
- Notebook with lyrics
- Raw editing style works well
