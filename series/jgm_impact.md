# JGM Impact

## Purpose
Show what Joshua Generation Ministries is doing in the community.

This is a trust-building, local-impact series that helps the audience see the mission behind the music.

## Format
- Monthly recap or impact mini-update
- Volunteer highlight
- Community story or service recap

## Best Hooks
- "This is what real ministry looks like."
- "The church isn’t just a building. It’s a mission."
- "When faith moves into action, this is what happens."

## CTA
Comment to join, volunteer, or share the next community update.

## Visual Style
- People-first footage
- Community, outreach, service moments
- Warm, close, human-centered visuals
