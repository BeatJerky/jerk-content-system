# Out Of The Trunk: 4-Week Rollout Plan

## Overview
This 4-week plan launches "Out Of The Trunk" as J.E.R.K.'s signature series, establishing it as a recognizable brand within the broader content system.

The rollout is designed to:
- build anticipation and series recognition
- test solo vs. guest episode formats
- establish optimal posting cadence and times
- generate audience requests for locations and guests
- network with other Christian artists and local creatives
- drive cross-platform engagement

---

## Week 1: Series Launch & Solo Foundation

### Goal
Introduce the series concept, establish the visual brand, prove the format works with solo episodes.

### Content Plan

#### Monday: Series Announcement Teaser
- **Platform**: Instagram Stories + TikTok + YouTube Shorts
- **Content**: 15-second teaser of the trunk opening
- **Hook**: "Something new is coming. Out Of The Trunk."
- **CTA**: "Follow for the full premiere tomorrow."
- **Metrics to watch**: story taps, shares, saves

#### Tuesday: Episode 1 — Compton Sunset
- **Platform**: All four (staggered by 2-4 hours)
- **Format**: Solo verse, 45-60 seconds
- **Location**: Iconic Compton spot (e.g., Centinela Park, Rosecrans Ave, local landmark)
- **Hook**: "Out Of The Trunk in Compton — this is where the message lives."
- **Script structure**:
  - 5-second intro (location + vibe)
  - 40-45 second verse
  - 5-second outro with CTA
- **CTA**: "Comment the next city spot we should hit."
- **Posting times**:
  - TikTok: 6:30 AM
  - Instagram: 8:00 AM
  - YouTube Shorts: 2:00 PM
  - Facebook: 12:00 PM
- **Captions**:
  - TikTok: Short and punchy. "The trunk is the stage. Compton is the city."
  - Instagram: Conversational. "This is where faith meets the streets. Out Of The Trunk starts today."
  - YouTube: Searchable. "Out Of The Trunk in Compton — J.E.R.K. Christian rap performance."
  - Facebook: Community-focused. "Bringing the message from Compton to the world. Comment the next spot."
- **Hashtags**: #OutOfTheTrunk #Compton #ChristianRap #FaithBeforeFame #ComptownRaps #Gospel #JERKMusic
- **Expected metrics**: 500-2K views per platform, 20-50 follows per platform

#### Wednesday: Repurposed Content
- **Format**: Carousel post (Instagram) or listicle (Facebook)
- **Content**: "5 Things About Out Of The Trunk Series"
  - 1. The trunk is the stage
  - 2. The city is the backdrop
  - 3. Faith is the message
  - 4. Community is the why
  - 5. You pick the next location
- **CTA**: "Save this and comment your location."
- **Platform**: Instagram + Facebook

#### Thursday: Behind-the-Scenes
- **Format**: Short video or story series
- **Content**: How we shot Episode 1 (setup, location scouting, audio check)
- **Hook**: "Here's how we made the trunk the stage."
- **CTA**: "Follow for more BTS content."
- **Platform**: Instagram Stories (5-7 slides) + TikTok (30-45 second video)

#### Friday: Engagement Reply
- **Format**: Video reply to top comments
- **Content**: Respond to location requests
- **Hook**: "You asked for [location]. We're locked in."
- **CTA**: "Drop another location below."
- **Platform**: Instagram + TikTok (reply videos)

#### Saturday: Community Highlight
- **Format**: Text post or light carousel
- **Content**: Feature Joshua Generation Ministries
- **Hook**: "This series is built on faith. This is our why."
- **CTA**: "Comment 'serve' if you want to get involved."
- **Platform**: Facebook + Instagram

#### Sunday: Week Recap
- **Format**: Story or short post
- **Content**: "Week 1 of Out Of The Trunk is live. Here's what's next."
- **CTA**: "Subscribe to see Episode 2."
- **Platform**: Instagram Stories + YouTube Community (if available)

### Week 1 Metrics Goal
- Total views: 5K-10K across platforms
- Follows gained: 100-200
- Comments with location requests: 20+
- Comments with guest suggestions: 10+

## Week 3: First Guest Collaboration

### Goal
Launch the guest format, test cross-promotion, expand reach through collaboration.

### Content Plan

#### Monday: Guest Announcement
- **Platform**: All four
- **Format**: Announcement video + story carousel
- **Content**: "[Guest Name] is pulling up Out Of The Trunk on Wednesday."
- **Hook**: "This collaboration is about to change things."
- **CTA**: "Follow [guest name]. This is going to be special."
- **Tag strategy**: Tag guest in caption, stories, and comments
- **Cross-post**: Share to guest's audience via tagged post (if they repost)
- **Expected reach boost**: 20-40% increase from guest's followers

#### Tuesday: BTS of Setup
- **Format**: Story series or short video
- **Content**: Prepping the location, mic check, guest arrival
- **Hook**: "The trunk is ready. The guest is locked in."
- **CTA**: "Watch the full episode tomorrow."
- **Platform**: Instagram Stories + TikTok

#### Wednesday: Guest Episode — Full Version
- **Platform**: YouTube (long-form), then repurposed to others
- **Format**: 5-8 minutes (full collab with intro/outro)
- **Location**: Scenic or symbolic Compton/Long Beach spot
- **Script structure**:
  - 30-second intro (introduce guest, explain location)
  - J.E.R.K. verse (45-60 seconds)
  - Guest verse (45-60 seconds)
  - Optional exchange or freestyle (30-60 seconds)
  - 30-second outro (cross-promote, CTA)
- **Hook**: "Out Of The Trunk with [guest name] — this is where faith-based artists connect."
- **Captions**:
  - YouTube: Full description with guest bio, links, stream links
  - Instagram Reel: "[Guest name] pulled up. This is what happens."
  - TikTok: "When real artists collaborate."
  - Facebook: "Check out [guest name]. New music coming."
- **CTAs**:
  - YouTube: Subscribe + stream guest's track
  - Instagram: Follow both accounts
  - TikTok: Follow both + tag the next guest
  - Facebook: Comment + share + follow both
- **Posting schedule**:
  - YouTube: 3:00 PM (full version)
  - Instagram: 8:00 AM (30-60 second clip)
  - TikTok: 7:00 PM (best clip)
  - Facebook: 12:00 PM (clip + guest feature)
- **Expected metrics**: 2K-5K views per platform, 100-200 follows, high shares

#### Thursday: Short Clip from Guest Episode
- **Format**: 15-30 second highlight
- **Content**: Best exchange or freestyle moment
- **Hook**: "The best 30 seconds."
- **CTA**: "Watch the full collab [link]."
- **Platform**: TikTok + Instagram Stories

#### Friday: Guest Music Feature
- **Format**: Carousel or listicle
- **Content**: "Where to Stream [Guest Name]'s Music"
  - Spotify link
  - Apple Music link
  - YouTube link
  - SoundCloud (if applicable)
- **Hook**: "If you vibed with [guest name], here's their music."
- **CTA**: "Stream and follow."
- **Platform**: Instagram + Facebook

#### Saturday: Audience Vote for Next Guest
- **Format**: Story poll or post carousel
- **Content**: "Who should pull up next? [Option 1] [Option 2] [Option 3]"
- **Hook**: "You pick the next guest."
- **Purpose**: Build audience investment, gather intel on network
- **Platform**: Instagram Stories + Facebook

#### Sunday: Week Recap
- **Format**: Post or story
- **Content**: "Week 3: The first guest. Here's what's next."
- **CTA**: "Subscribe for more."

### Week 3 Metrics Goal
- Total views: 10K-25K (guest boost)
- Follows gained: 300-600
- Cross-platform traffic: 30-50% increase
- Guest channel traffic: 100-300 click-throughs
- Video completion rate: 65-75%
- Collaboration ROI: measure guest follower growth

## Post-Week 4: Sustainable Cadence

### Recommended Recurring Schedule

**Weekly Format:**
- **Monday or Tuesday**: Solo episode (location-based)
- **Wednesday or Thursday**: Guest collaboration (every other week) OR community/faith episode
- **Friday or Saturday**: Audience request fulfillment or freestyle
- **Sunday**: Recap or preview

**Monthly Format:**
- Week 1: Solo episodes + series intro
- Week 2: Solo variants + audience requests
- Week 3: Guest collaboration
- Week 4: Faith/ministry focus + roadmap preview

### Content Pillars for Out Of The Trunk
- Solo performances: 40%
- Guest collaborations: 30%
- Faith/ministry content: 20%
- Audience fulfillment: 10%

### Platform Distribution
- **TikTok**: 2-3 episodes per week (raw energy, highest frequency)
- **Instagram Reels**: 1-2 per week (polished, community-focused)
- **YouTube Shorts**: 1-2 per week (searchable, long-form link)
- **Facebook**: 1-2 per week (community and Joshua Generation focus)

### Metrics Targets (Ongoing)
- Views: 15K-50K per week across platforms
- Follows: 200-400 per week
- Guest collaborations: 2-3 per month
- Location requests fulfilled: 1-2 per week
- Engagement rate: 6-10%
- Series subscriber growth: 50-100 per week
