# Bars & Bible

## Purpose
One verse. One bar. One truth.

This series ties scripture to the actual craft of songwriting, helping the audience see how faith informs the music.

## Format
- Weekly short video or reel
- 15–60 seconds
- Use a scripture reference + lyric or insight
- Include one personal sentence on what the verse taught you

## Best Hooks
- "This verse changed how I write."
- "One Bible verse changed my whole flow."
- "This is the scripture I keep returning to."

## CTA
Comment your favorite verse or tag someone who needs this message.

## Visual Style
- Close-up talking head
- Bible or notebook in frame
- Optional lyric overlay with the verse
