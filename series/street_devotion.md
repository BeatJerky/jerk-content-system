# Street Devotion

## Purpose
Deliver a devotional or faith-based message in public space.

This is a strong series for connecting faith with the real city, people, and location.

## Format
- On-location devotional
- 20–90 seconds
- Use city textures, street footage, and a direct message

## Best Hooks
- "If you're carrying heavy stuff, stop scrolling."
- "This is what I learned walking through the city with God."
- "God was in the middle of my mess."

## CTA
Share this with someone who needs encouragement.

## Visual Style
- On-location with city movement in the background
- Quiet but direct narrative
- Minimal but textured overlays
