# Compton Testimony

## Purpose
Share the real life story behind the music and ministry.

This is where J.E.R.K.'s authenticity lives. It creates emotional trust and makes the audience feel seen.

## Format
- Biweekly testimony story
- 1–8 minute long-form or short-form breakdown
- Focus on a moment of pain, pressure, faith, or breakthrough

## Best Hooks
- "I almost walked away from faith..."
- "This is the moment God changed my direction."
- "Nobody in Compton told me this about faith."

## CTA
Share this with someone who needs hope or comment your own testimony.

## Visual Style
- On-location footage
- Archive photos or street footage
- Strong direct-to-camera speaking
- Honest, vulnerable pacing
