## Script Template: Any Solo Episode

### Structure (60 seconds total)

```
[INTRO — 5 seconds]
"Out Of The Trunk at [location].
[One sentence about why this location matters or what the verse is about]."

[VERSE — 50 seconds]
[One complete verse or freestyle]

[OUTRO — 5 seconds]
"[Series tagline or CTA].
Next up: [tease]."
```

### Verse Content Guidelines
- Hook should establish faith + location identity
- Content should be authentic to real struggle or message
- Delivery should be confident and clear
- Message should invite the audience into something real, not preach at them

### CTA Options
- Comment the next location
- Tag a rapper who should pull up
- Stream the full track
- Comment if you relate to this message
- Share with someone who needs this

---

## General Direction for All Episodes

1. **Energy**: Authentic and grounded, not overly polished
2. **Message**: Faith + community + Compton identity should always be present
3. **Delivery**: Conversational and real, like you're talking to a friend
4. **Purpose**: Each episode should make the audience feel something or think something new
5. **Community**: Always leave space for audience participation (comments, tags, requests)
