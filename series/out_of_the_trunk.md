# Out Of The Trunk

## Purpose
J.E.R.K. performs freestyled or scripted bars directly from the trunk of his car at iconic Compton and Long Beach locations.

This series is raw, energetic, and creates a platform for:
- authentic street performance
- local landmark visibility
- guest rapper collaborations
- community connection through music
- viral-ready short-form content

## Format
- Location-based performance video
- 15–90 seconds (short-form) or 3–8 minutes (long-form with guest)
- Trunk as stage, city backdrop as context
- High energy, professional audio if possible, but raw is acceptable

## Series Structure

### Solo Episodes
- J.E.R.K. performs one complete verse or freestyle
- Location intro: "Out Of The Trunk at [location]"
- Strong hook about the location or the bars
- Performance with city energy in the background

### Guest Episodes
- J.E.R.K. invites another Christian rapper or local artist
- Both perform a verse or trade bars
- Location-specific intro and outro
- Collaboration energy and mutual endorsement

## Best Hooks
- "Out Of The Trunk at [landmark] with [guest name]"
- "This verse was born in Compton. This is where it lives."
- "The trunk is the stage when the message is real."
- "[Guest name] pulled up and we made this happen."
- "Bringing the message straight from the car to the city."

## Content Types

### Type 1: Landmark Solo
- Location: major Compton or Long Beach landmark
- Content: one completed verse or freestyle
- Vibe: confident, rooted, authentic
- Audio: car sound system or external mic

### Type 2: Guest Collaboration
- Location: scenic or symbolic spot
- Content: exchange of verses or joint freestyle
- Vibe: energy, mutual respect, shared message
- Audio: both rappers mic'd

### Type 3: Church/Ministry Partner Solo
- Location: Joshua Generation Ministries or partner church
- Content: faith-centered verse or scripture-grounded bars
- Vibe: spiritual, community-focused
- Audio: clear delivery

### Type 4: Street Freestyle
- Location: active street, market, or community gathering
- Content: improvised bars over beat
- Vibe: spontaneous, raw, interactive
- Audio: natural with beat

## Potential Guest Rappers
- other Christian hip-hop artists
- local Compton/Long Beach rappers
- Joshua Generation Ministries partners
- youth from the community
- faith-based creatives

## Outreach Strategy

### How to invite guests
1. DM them and tell them the concept
2. Explain the location and timing
3. Offer to feature their music or ministry
4. Cross-promote to both audiences
5. Use the collaboration to strengthen community ties

### Cross-promotion
- feature guest's music in pinned comment
- tag their page in the post
- do a response video where guest posts from their car
- create a mini-series of guest appearances

## Visual Style
- trunk lit well (daylight or LED lights)
- city backdrop or landmark visible
- wide shots showing location context
- close-ups of delivery and energy
- text overlay: "Out Of The Trunk" branding
- lower third with location name and guest name

## Audio Requirements
- clear vocal delivery
- beat should be recognizable
- minimal wind noise
- record a clean backup mic take when possible

## CTA Options
- "Comment the next location we should hit"
- "Tag a rapper who should pull up next"
- "Stream the full track [link]"
- "Who's next? Let's get you on the trunk."
- "This is [guest name] — follow them [link]"
