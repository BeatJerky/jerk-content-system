# Out Of The Trunk: Guest Outreach Strategy

## Purpose
Build a network of Christian rappers, local artists, and faith-based creatives who can collaborate on Out Of The Trunk episodes, expanding reach and building community.

---

## Phase 1: Identify Potential Guests (Week 1-2)

### Tier 1: High Priority (Established Christian Hip-Hop Artists)
- Reach: 10K-100K followers
- Influence: Strong in Christian rap community
- Fit: Faith-first artists, ministry-minded, community-focused

### Tier 2: Mid-Tier (Local Compton/Long Beach Artists)
- Reach: 1K-10K followers
- Influence: Respected locally, community presence
- Fit: Open to faith conversations, collaborative spirit

### Tier 3: Emerging (Up-and-coming Christian Artists)
- Reach: 500-5K followers
- Influence: Passionate, growing, building authentically
- Fit: Hungry for collaboration, mission-aligned

### Tier 4: Community Partners
- Joshua Generation Ministries partners
- Local churches and youth leaders
- Community organizers
- Fit: Aligned with mission, want visibility

## Phase 3: Guest Confirmation & Logistics

### Once They Say Yes

1. **Schedule**: Suggest 2-3 date/time options
2. **Location**: Propose a meaningful spot (ask if they have preferences)
3. **Format**: Confirm solo verse or verse exchange
4. **Audio**: Ask if they have a beat or if they want to use one of yours
5. **Content**: Discuss loose theme or message
6. **Cross-promo**: Confirm they'll share to their audience

### Confirmation Message Template

```
Yes! Let's make this happen.

Here's the plan:
- Location: [location]
- Date: [date] at [time]
- Format: You perform a verse, I perform a verse, we can freestyle if the energy is right
- We film it and release across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook
- I'll tag you everywhere and feature your music
- You do the same for your audience

Bring:
- Your energy
- Your beat (optional, or use mine)
- Good vibes

Questions?
```

## Phase 4: Post-Episode Promotion

### Day of Release
- Tag guest in caption

- Cross-post to Facebook Groups and communities

### Day 2-3
- Reply to comments mentioning guest
- Create a separate post featuring guest's music/links
- Engage with guest's repost to their audience

### Week 1
- Create a follow-up post: "Where [Guest] Should Pull Up Next"
- Drive audience to guest's profile and music
- Measure cross-platform traffic and follower gain for both parties

## Success Metrics for Guest Episodes

### Per Guest Collaboration
- **Reach**: 20-40% increase over typical solo episode
- **Follows**: 50-150 new follows (attributable to guest)
- **Cross-traffic**: Guest channel traffic increases 50-100 visitors
- **Engagement**: Comments and shares higher than usual
- **Guest channel growth**: Guest gains 20-50 followers from exposure

### Over Time (Month 1)
- **Collaborations**: 2-3 guest episodes
- **Network**: 5-10 artist relationships strengthened or initiated
- **Audience**: Reach expands into guest audiences (cumulative 5K-15K new reach)
- **Authority**: Series becomes known as collaborative platform

## Long-Term Guest Strategy

### Month 1-2
- 2-3 guest collaborations
- Focus on quality over quantity
- Build genuine relationships

### Month 2-3
- 1 guest per week
- Mix Tier 1 (bigger names), Tier 2 (local), and Tier 3 (emerging)
- Build reputation as platform for Christian artists

### Month 3+
- Sustain 1 guest collab every 2 weeks
- Rotate between tiers
- Leverage guest momentum to reach new audiences
- Create "Out Of The Trunk Collective" feeling

---

## Red Flags & Best Practices

### Good Fit
- ✓ Artist is mission-aligned (faith-centered)
- ✓ Energy matches the concept (authentic, grounded)
- ✓ They're willing to cross-promote
- ✓ They see it as collaboration, not just exposure

### Poor Fit
- ✗ Artist is only focused on self-promotion
- ✗ Content doesn't align with faith values
- ✗ They won't commit to cross-promo
- ✗ They have negative community reputation

### Best Practices
1. Reach out personally, not as a mass message
2. Explain why you specifically want them
3. Make it easy to say yes (simple process)
4. Deliver what you promise (tag them, promote them)
5. Build genuine relationship, not transactional
6. Follow up after episode (thank them, share metrics)
7. Leave door open for future collabs
