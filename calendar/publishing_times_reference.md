# Publishing Time Reference

## Best Platform Posting Times (PST)

### Instagram
- Monday: 8:00 AM
- Tuesday: 7:00 AM
- Wednesday: 8:00 AM
- Thursday: 9:00 AM
- Friday: 8:00 AM
- Saturday: 12:00 PM
- Sunday: 2:00 PM

### TikTok
- Best windows: 6:00–10:00 AM
- Evening: 7:00–11:00 PM
- Best for raw content and discovery

### YouTube Shorts
- Weekdays: 2:00–4:00 PM
- Weekends: 9:00–11:00 AM

### Facebook
- Wednesday: 9:00 AM–3:00 PM
- Weekends: 10:00 AM–6:00 PM
- Monday: 9:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 7:00 PM

## Staggering Rule

Do not post the same clip on all four platforms at the same time.

Stagger by 2–4 hours when possible.

## Social Notes

- Prioritize Facebook for community and non-profit content
- Prioritize TikTok for raw or unpolished content
- Prioritize Instagram for polished music content
- Prioritize YouTube for long-form and educational content
- Leave Sunday lighter, with 1–2 posts maximum

## Story Slots

Use 2–3 story slots per day across Instagram and Facebook.

Good options:
- behind-the-scenes
- local scenery
- question prompt
- countdown
- quick devotional
- volunteer update
- music studio update
