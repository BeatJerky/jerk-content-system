# Voice and Identity

## Core Voice
J.E.R.K.'s voice should be:
- direct
- real
- biblical but not preachy
- grounded in Compton culture and community
- honest about pain, faith, and purpose

## Tone Rules
- No fake church language
- No jargon-heavy motivational speak
- No polished “brand voice” tone
- Speak like a real man with a real message

## Examples of the Right Tone
- "I almost walked away from faith..."
- "This is what God taught me in the middle of the mess."
- "I stopped trying to sound like everybody else."

## Examples of the Wrong Tone
- "We are thrilled to announce..."
- "It is my honor to share..."
- "In today’s digital landscape..."

## Identity Anchors
- Christian rap
- Compton
- community leadership
- faith through struggle
- music with substance
- Joshua Generation Ministries
