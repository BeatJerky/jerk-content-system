# Hook Formulas

In short-form content, the hook is the new thumbnail. It determines whether someone stops scrolling.

## Formula 1: The Contrarian
"Most Christian rappers are doing this backwards..."

## Formula 2: The Mistake / Problem
"The reason your music isn't reaching people isn't what you think."

## Formula 3: The Audience Call-Out
"If you're a Christian creative feeling stuck, stop scrolling."

## Formula 4: The Story Hook
"I wrote this bar after almost giving up on music..."

## Formula 5: The Process / Value Promise
"3 things I learned writing Christian bars in Compton."

## Formula 6: The Pattern Interrupt
"Stop scrolling. This verse changes everything."

## Formula 7: The Proof-Led Hook
"This format kept showing up in my best-performing videos, so I rebuilt it."

## Rule
Ask: what does the viewer get in exchange for their attention?

If the answer isn’t clear in the first 3 seconds, rewrite the hook.
