# Content Pillars

These are the six pillars the content should rotate through over time.

## 1) Music & Performance (30%)
- song snippets
- studio session clips
- live performances
- behind-the-bar content
- lyric breakdowns

## 2) Faith & Testimony (25%)
- personal testimony
- devotional moments
- scripture breakdowns
- prayer clips
- faith-first perspective

## 3) Community & Non-Profit Impact (20%)
- Joshua Generation Ministries events
- outreach recap
- volunteer stories
- community moments
- ministry highlight

## 4) Behind-the-Scenes & Process (10%)
- writing workflow
- studio setup
- production process
- day in the life

## 5) Opinion & Teaching (10%)
- industry commentary
- creator advice
- scripture teaching
- practical lessons for faith and art

## 6) Fan Participation & Engagement (5%)
- Q&A
- comments-to-video
- story polls
- community prompts

## Rotation Rule
Do not try to cover all six every week. Instead:
- focus on 2–3 pillars per week
- make sure all six appear in a 4–6 week cycle
- avoid repeating the same pillar too much in a row
