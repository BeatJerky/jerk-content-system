# Operational Rhythm

## Weekly Operating Structure

### Monday
- Review tracker
- Review last week’s performance
- Set the week’s core long-form topic
- Generate 10–15 content ideas

### Tuesday
- Script the long-form asset
- Film core footage and B-roll
- Capture local environment and community context

### Wednesday
- Edit and repurpose
- Produce 8–12 short-form clips
- Draft captions and hooks
- Schedule the content

### Thursday–Sunday
- Publish on schedule
- Engage with audience in comments and DMs
- Reply with video when appropriate
- Track and collect metrics

## Sustainable Production Rule
Do not attempt to create every piece of content from scratch. Build a repeatable library of:
- hooks
- story formats
- B-roll footage
- recurring series templates
- key scripture and teaching references

## Engagement Rule
Spend 30 minutes per day engaging with comments and community. This helps build return viewers and creates content ideas from the audience.
