# J.E.R.K. Social Content Operating System

The One-Machine Content Blueprint for J.E.R.K., a Christian rap artist and community leader rooted in Compton, CA.

This repo contains a complete 4-week content system built around the idea that one weekly core recording session can be repurposed into 30–45 social posts across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook.

## Purpose

This system is designed to help J.E.R.K.:
- build consistent visibility
- connect with faith-based and community audiences
- turn one recording session into a multi-platform content engine
- create repeatable series that build audience retention
- keep outreach, testimony, and music unified under one system

## Core Strategy

Stop thinking about four platforms. Think about one machine with four exhaust pipes.

One core asset becomes many platform-native posts:
- 1 long-form video or testimony
- 8–12 short-form vertical clips
- 2–3 carousels or text posts
- 3–5 stories
- 1 collaborative post

Total: 30–45 pieces from one recording session.

## Repository Structure

- `tracker/` — weekly content trackers and master tracker
- `calendar/` — platform-specific posting schedules
- `prompts/` — AI prompt templates for planning, scripting, repurposing, captions
- `series/` — recurring series definitions and examples
- `docs/` — brand voice, pillars, hooks, visuals, rhythm
- `analytics/` — KPI dashboard and weekly review structure

## The 4-Week Content Plan

### Week 1: Launch & Foundation
- Introduce the voice and mission
- Launch recurring series (Bars & Bible, Behind the Bar, Compton Testimony)
- Strong faith + music + community blend

### Week 2: Faith & Testimony
- Personal testimony, devotion, and spiritual lessons
- Higher vulnerability and outreach language
- Install audience trust and connection

### Week 3: Music & Impact
- Performance clips, outreach recaps, community storytelling
- Highlight JGM and local impact
- Bring music and mission together

### Week 4: Teaching & Momentum
- Practical teaching, opinion, and growth content
- Build authority and audience retention
- Invite community participation and conversation

## The Operating Rhythm

- Monday: plan the week
- Tuesday: record and script core asset
- Wednesday: edit and repurpose
- Thursday-Sunday: publish, engage, and track results

## Content Tracker Lifecycle

Idea → Scripted → Filmed → Edited → Scheduled → Published → Measured → Repurpose/Archive

## Quick Start

1. Read `QUICK_START.md`
2. Open the tracker for the current week
3. Use the planning prompt in `prompts/weekly_planning_prompt.md`
4. Script the core content
5. Repurpose into clips
6. Schedule using the matching file in `calendar/`
7. Update metrics and repeat
