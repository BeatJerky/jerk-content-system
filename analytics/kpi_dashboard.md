# KPI Dashboard

## Primary Metrics
Track these 24–72 hours after posting:
- Reach
- Views
- Watch time
- Saves
- Shares
- Comments
- Follows gained

## Secondary Metrics
- Average watch time
- Watch-through rate
- Follower conversion rate
- Story replies
- Comment quality
- DM conversion

## Priority Metric
Follower Conversion Rate = Follows Gained ÷ Reach × 100

## Why It Matters
A Reel with 100K views and 50 follows (0.05%) is less valuable than a Reel with 10K views and 100 follows (1%).

## Weekly Review Questions
- Which pillar performed best?
- Which content got saves and shares?
- Which hooks drove the most follows?
- Which content built community trust?
- Which posts should be repurposed or archived?
